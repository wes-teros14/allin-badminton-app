Pick a control, and pick where it lives.

Decided: the nav orb, in an Appearance row above Sign out on My Profile — built and shipped on 2026-09-05. This page is kept as the record of what was considered and why. Every control below is still live, so pressing one repaints the page in the light tokens that actually shipped.

01

Which control

Nav orb

One 44 × 44 icon button. The crescent fills into a disc and throws eight rays — a single element, not an icon swap. Small enough to fit anywhere.

Shuttle switch

76 × 40. The thumb doesn’t slide — it arcs, dips and tumbles end-over-end, the way a birdie actually flies. Starfield fades to warm sky beneath it.

Light / Auto / Dark

A full-width segment. The only one that can say “follow my phone”, which is what iOS and Android already schedule at sunset. Needs a whole row.
02

Where it lives

Nav bar, right-hand end

On every screen, for every signed-in user, one tap from anywhere. The slot is 44 px wide, so only the orb fits — and two states means it can never offer Auto.

Orb only

My Profile, above Sign out

A full-width “Appearance” row sitting with the other account settings, where people already go to change their nickname. Two taps away, fits any control.

Any control

Both — orb in the nav, full control in Profile

The orb is the quick flip from anywhere; the profile row owns the real setting, Auto included. One piece of state, two ways into it.

Recommended

Inside the hamburger menu

Tidy, and out of the way. But that dropdown renders behind hasAdminTabs — it exists only for admins and moderators, so ordinary players would have no way to reach the toggle at all.

    03

    What light mode costs, whichever you pick

    The :root block in src/index.css is shadcn’s stock light default with the brand purple dropped in. It has never once rendered, and four tokens break the moment it does.

    --muted swaps role between the two blocks

    Light defines --muted: #6B7280, a mid-grey text colour. Dark defines --muted: #1E1230, a surface. Every bg-muted in the app turns into a grey slab the first time light mode is used. A bug, not a taste call.

    --gold on white is 1.8 : 1

    #FFB200 against white measures 1.81 : 1, far under the 4.5 : 1 floor — the podium’s first place would vanish. Keep #FFB200 as the medal fill and add --gold-ink: #8A5A00 (5.94 : 1) for anything that is text.

    --destructive and --success both fail on white

    #DC595E hits 3.71 : 1 and #22C55E roughly 2.2 : 1. Darkened here for light mode only, to #B3252B (6.55 : 1) and #157F3D (5.09 : 1). Their dark-mode values are untouched.

    The nav bar is purple in both themes

    TopNavBar is bg-primary text-primary-foreground and --primary is #6F3E87 in both blocks — so the bar itself needs no light-mode work, but the orb must draw in currentColor, never var(--foreground), which would be 2.37 : 1 on that purple.

    --primary is safe in both

    #6F3E87 measures 7.69 : 1 against white and the same against its own white foreground. It carries across unchanged, which is why the app still looks like itself above.
