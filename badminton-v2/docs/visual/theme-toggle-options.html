Three ways to turn the lights on.

The app has been permanently dark since launch — index.html hard-codes class="dark". Every control below is live: press one and this whole sheet, including the phone preview, repaints in the proposed light tokens. Pick a variant, then it gets built for real.

01

Nav orb

TopNavBar · end of the bar
SessionsLeaderboard

44 × 44 target · 0 px of nav width

One object, two states. The crescent fills into a disc and throws eight rays. It is a single div driven by box-shadow — no icon swap, no crossfade, nothing to pop.

  • Costs the nav no horizontal space, which matters at 384 px.
  • Always reachable — no menu to open first.
  • Cannot express “follow my phone”. Two states only.
  • The icon shows the current theme, not the destination, so the action has to live in aria-label.
  • The nav is bg-primary in both themes, so the orb must be drawn in currentColor (white), never var(--foreground) — that would be 2.37 : 1 on the purple bar in light mode.
02

Shuttle switch

ProfileView · above Sign out

76 × 40 · the birdie arcs and flips

The one thing anyone will remember. The thumb does not slide across the track — it lifts, arcs and tumbles end-over-end, because that is what a shuttlecock actually does. Underneath, a starfield fades into a warm sky.

  • Unmistakably this app. No other product has this switch.
  • Still reads as a standard switch, so nobody has to learn it.
  • Needs ~76 px, so it wants a settings row, not the nav bar.
  • Motion is the whole point, so it must degrade cleanly under prefers-reduced-motion — it does.
03

Tri-state segment

ProfileView · above Sign out

Auto → follows your phone

The honest one. A two-state switch quietly lies: it cannot say “match my phone”, which is what most people actually want and what iOS and Android already schedule at sunset.

  • Only variant that supports prefers-color-scheme as a real choice.
  • Current state is written out — no icon to decode.
  • Wants a full row, so it lives in settings, not the nav.
  • Three states means three things to persist and test, not two.

Light mode is not free. The :root block in src/index.css is shadcn’s untouched default with the brand purple dropped in — it has never once rendered. Four tokens break the moment it does.

--muted swaps roles between themes

Light defines --muted: #6B7280, a mid-grey text colour. Dark defines --muted: #1E1230, a surface. Every bg-muted in the app becomes a slab of grey the moment the light block is used. A real bug, not a taste call.

--gold is unreadable on white — 1.8 : 1

#FFB200 against #FFFFFF measures 1.81 : 1, far under the 4.5 : 1 floor. First place on the podium would vanish. Proposed here: keep #FFB200 as the medal fill and add --gold-ink: #8A5A00 (5.94 : 1) for anything that is text.

--destructive and --success both fail on white

#DC595E hits 3.71 : 1 and #22C55E about 2.2 : 1 on white. Darkened here for light mode only, to #B3252B (6.55 : 1) and #157F3D (5.09 : 1). Their dark-mode values are untouched.

--muted-surface exists only in :root

Already flagged in the handoff as a live trap. Once light mode is real it stops being a trap and becomes an ordinary token — #F4F4F6 is fine, it just needs a .dark counterpart before anything uses it.

--primary is safe in both

#6F3E87 measures 7.69 : 1 against white, and the same against its own white foreground. It carries across unchanged, which is why the brand still reads as the brand in the preview above.
