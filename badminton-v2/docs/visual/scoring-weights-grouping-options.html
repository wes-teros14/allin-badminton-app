Scoring Weights — three ways to break up the 12-item grid

MatchGeneratorPanel.tsx:615-683 renders all 12 checkbox+number weight pairs in one flat 2-column grid with no grouping — a >4-option decision point the critique flagged for both cognitive load and the mis-tap risk on the (now-enlarged) checkboxes. All three mocks group the same 12 weights into the same four families: Fairness & Pacing, Gender Rules, Rest & Opening, Rewards. Scroll the phone frames; click summaries/toggles to expand.

Option A

Labeled sections, everything still visible

Same flat scroll as today, but a small uppercase category caption breaks the 12 into four groups of 2-4. Zero new interactions to learn, and nothing is hidden — but the >4-visible- options problem isn't actually solved, it's just organized. Best for an admin who wants to eyeball everything at a glance before Generate.

Option B

Each family collapsed, open only if tuned

Same four families, but each is its own <details>, closed by default. A group auto-opens (and shows a count badge) only when one of its weights differs from DEFAULTS — the same “a pin set but out of sight” principle already used for Fixed Opening Games. A typical week where only Rest & Opening was touched shows 2 open groups and 2 closed ones, directly solving the >4-visible-options problem.

Option C

Common vs. Advanced, two tiers only

Skips categories entirely. The 2 weights this admin actually adjusts (repeatPartnerPenalty, restSpacingPenalty — per the critique's own question about usage data) sit inline, always visible. The other 10 live behind one “Advanced” toggle. Fewest decisions in the common case (2, well under the working-memory limit) but a real tuning session needs the toggle open anyway, and doesn't group the advanced 10 at all.
