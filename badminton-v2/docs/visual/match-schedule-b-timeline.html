What it fixes

  • The night gets a shape. The filled spine is the progress bar — you read “roughly halfway” from the geometry, without a number.
  • Order is never broken. Game 14 is exactly where you expect it, between 13 and 15. If you think in game numbers, nothing moves.
  • Court is a colour, not a word. A 20 px badge per row costs almost nothing and makes “court 2 is running behind” visible by pattern.
  • One expanded card per court, everything else one line. Twenty games fit in about two screens instead of six.
  • Winners recorded in the same row, so the played half becomes a record of the night rather than a grey wall.

Trade-offs

  • Queued games have no court yet — a court is only assigned when the admin sends the game on. So two thirds of the badges read –. That is honest, but it is a lot of placeholders. Option A avoids this by only promising “first open court”.
  • The rail eats 42 px of a 384 px screen — about 11 % of the width, permanently.
  • You still scroll past everything played to reach what is next, unless the view auto-scrolls to the live node on open. Option A puts live at the top instead.

Correction I earlier called the ← My Matches link a bug. The link round-trips through the picker and lands on your own schedule. I traced the route table but not the redirect.

Court colour Court 2 uses a teal that is not currently in the palette (#1F6F6B light, #4FD1C5 dark). If you want to stay strictly on brand, the alternative is two tints of the purple — which is calmer, but much harder to tell apart at 20 px.
