Match edit — three ways to swap players without the “already in this match” detour

Today each of the four dropdowns disables anyone already in the match, so moving Bogs to Team 2 means parking a stranger in his slot first. The guard is right (four different players) but it blocks the one edit admins actually make. Every mock starts as Bogs & Wes vs John & Steph; the task is to swap Bogs and Steph. The result line under each shows what would be saved. Reset mock starts over.

Option A

Choosing a teammate swaps instead of blocking

Same four dropdowns. A player already in the match is no longer disabled — the option reads “Steph  ⇆ swap”, and picking it puts Steph here and sends Bogs to Steph’s old slot. Nothing new to learn, one tap per swap, and the four-different-players rule can never be broken because a swap keeps the set intact. Substitutions work exactly as before.

Try: in Team 1’s first dropdown pick Steph ⇆ swap.

Option B

Tap one slot, then another, to swap them

The four slots are tappable rows with a ⇆ handle. Tap Bogs (it lights up gold), tap Steph (teal targets), they trade places. Tap the name text instead to open the roster for a substitute. Explicit and obvious once seen, but it is a second gesture next to the dropdown, and a half-finished swap needs a way out (tap the same slot again, or Cancel).

Try: tap Bogs, then tap Steph.

Tap a slot to start a swap, or tap a name to substitute.

Option C

Pick the pairing; dropdowns only for substitutes

Four players can only be split 2 v 2 in three ways, so the form shows those three as rows with their level totals and the current one selected. Any cross-team swap is one tap on a row — “swap Bogs and Steph” is just the row where Bogs & John face Wes & Steph. The dropdowns move below, used only to bring someone new in. Most direct for the common case; the form gets taller.

Try: tap the Bogs & John vs Wes & Steph row.
