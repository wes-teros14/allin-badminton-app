Cheers tab — three ways to bring the switcher under 4 options

CheersLeaderboard (LeaderboardView.tsx:526-547) renders all 6 categories from CHEER_CATEGORIES in one grid-cols-6 row — the critique's one flagged >4-visible-options decision point on this page. Worth noting in passing: the chip labels are text-[8px], below even the 11px functional-text floor other pages were fixed to — each option below also corrects that. Click a category in each mock.

Option A

Two rows of three

Simplest change: same chips, same one-tap selection, just wrapped to a 3×2 grid instead of 1×6. All six stay visible and reachable in one glance — but the guideline is about simultaneous options, not layout, so this organizes the six without actually reducing how many a player has to weigh at once.

Ranked by share of each player's cheers

Option B

Scrollable strip, ~4 visible

One row, but wider chips that only fit about 4 at a time, with a fade edge hinting more exist off-screen (the same horizontal-scroll pattern MySessionsView's Past Sessions already avoids elsewhere in the app, so this reintroduces a pattern the project has otherwise steered away from). Genuinely caps what's visible at once, at the cost of a swipe to reach the last two.

Ranked by share of each player's cheers

Option C

Single dropdown

Replaces the chip row with one Category: Offense ▾ select — exactly one visible option at a time, the strictest read of the guideline. Cheapest on space and the only one that scales cleanly past 6 categories, but loses the at-a-glance emoji/color identity the chips give each category, and swapping categories now costs opening a picker instead of one tap.
