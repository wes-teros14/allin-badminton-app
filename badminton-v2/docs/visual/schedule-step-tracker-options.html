Schedule tracker — three ways to fix the compound step

NoScheduleYet's step 3 reads “Registration closes · matches created” (MatchBoard.tsx:300) and stays fully unchecked until matches exist — even once registration has already closed, which the session-card badge (MySessionsView.tsx) has already told the player separately. All three mocks let you toggle Registration: closed on/off to see how each option represents that in-between state.

Option A

Split into two real steps

“Registration closes” and “Matches created” become two separate, independently-checkable steps — five total instead of four. Most literal fix: nothing is ever half-true. Costs one more row in an already-tall empty state, and the tracker now spends two steps on something that happens automatically seconds apart in practice.

Option B

Half-filled marker for the in-between state

Keeps the original four steps and wording, but the step-3 marker itself gets a third visual state — a half-filled ring, distinct from the empty circle and the solid checkmark — plus a small sub-label naming exactly what's still pending. No new rows, no reworded copy, but it's a new visual state to design and explain (a half-circle isn't self-evident without the sub-label).

Option C

Drop the compound clause entirely

Simplest fix: reword step 3 to just “Matches created” and let the session-card badge be the only place that reports registration status (it already does this today, so nothing is lost, just de-duplicated). Four steps stay four steps, no new visual state — but the tracker no longer explains why matches aren't ready yet if a player never saw the card badge.
