Fixed Opening Games — three ways to hide it by default

Pinning is rare, so the two rows should not sit in the middle of the Settings panel every week. In every mock the section starts hidden; open it, tick a Pin, and watch how each option signals “something is pinned” once it is closed again — a hidden pin that silently fixes game 1 is the failure to design against.

Option A

Collapsible block inside Settings, like Scoring Weights

Stays where it is, but becomes a <details> that is closed by default — the same treatment Scoring Weights already gets at the bottom of the panel. The summary line carries a gold 2 pinned chip whenever pins exist, and the block opens itself on load if any are set. Smallest change, consistent with the panel’s own idiom.

Option B

Behind an “Advanced” switch at the top of Settings

Settings gains one checkbox, Show advanced, off by default. Off, the panel shows only Match Rules and Optimizer; on, it also shows Fixed Opening Games and Scoring Weights — the two things a normal week never touches. The switch remembers itself in localStorage. Cleanest panel, but a pin can only be seen after flipping the switch, so the count also appears next to the switch.

Option C

A one-line link under the Generate button, outside Settings

Nothing in Settings at all. Under the Generate button sits a quiet text link, Pin opening games…, that expands the two rows inline right above where you act. Once anything is pinned the line reads 2 opening games pinned · edit in gold, so it is visible without opening anything. Most discoverable at the moment of use; costs one text line always.
