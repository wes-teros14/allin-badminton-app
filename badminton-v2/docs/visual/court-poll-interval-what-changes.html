Court check: 5 seconds to 10 seconds, what changes on screen

Player phones and the courtside tablet learn about court changes two ways at once: a live push from Supabase, and a check every 5 seconds that re-reads the courts whether or not anything changed. This page shows which parts of the screen use which, and what a slower (or push-only) check would and would not change.

4. What you could actually notice

  • Normal nightNo difference The push arrives about a second after the admin taps Finish, so the court cards flip at the same moment under all three options. The check never gets to matter.
  • A push is lost while "connected"Only real cost Today the 5-second check repairs the card within 5 s. At 10 s within 10 s, at 30 s within 30 s. With push-only, nothing repairs it until the next game change, a page reload, or the app coming back to the foreground. This is the one case where push-only is visibly worse.
  • Live connection dropsSame under all The "Reconnecting…" badge appears. Options 1 and 2 keep checking. Push-only starts checking every 5 s at that moment. On reconnect the app refreshes once to catch up, which is already built.
  • Someone edits their name or avatarSlightly later Names and avatars are cached for 60 seconds, and only re-read at a refresh. Today and 10 s: within about a minute. Push-only: at the next refresh after the cache expires, so possibly the next game change.
  • Phone comes back to the foregroundNo difference Refreshes immediately on return, separate from the check. Unchanged.
  • On-court timer, admin Finish buttons, "Your next game"Not affected The timer is a local 1 s tick. The admin page has its own hooks. "Your next game" already uses push only.
  • Courtside tabletNeeds one extra change Options 1 and 2 are a one-line change. Push-only needs the live-push status passed into the court hook, and the tablet currently throws that status away (it also never shows a connection warning).
What this will not fix Fewer reads help a small database, but the live push is not free: every change makes Supabase check each listener's permissions, and each phone keeps several channels open. The memory chart showed the database swapping to disk before anything broke, so a bigger instance is still the real headroom. Treat this change as reducing load, not as a guarantee against another outage.

5. Where the code lives

  • src/hooks/useCourtState.ts lines 114-136: the 5 s check and the foreground refresh.
  • src/hooks/useRealtime.ts: the live push, the status (connected, reconnecting, disconnected) and the catch-up refresh on reconnect.
  • src/views/LiveBoardView.tsx, PlayerView.tsx, SessionPlayerDetailView.tsx: the three screens that use both.
